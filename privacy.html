Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Aether Interiors & Furnishings ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website, why, and what rights you have over it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us through the "Start a Project" form:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number, so we can reply to you
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Anything you write in the project-details field (optional)

We do not collect this information any other way. We don't run analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising scripts on this site.

We do not use cookies. This website does not set any cookies on your device, and does not use cookie-based tracking or advertising of any kind.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry about interior design, custom furniture, or related services — for example, to call, email, or WhatsApp you back about your project.

How it's used and stored

When you submit the form, your browser opens your own email app with these details already filled in, addressed to us — nothing is sent to our servers automatically. We only receive it once you choose to send that email yourself. Once we do receive it, we keep it only for as long as needed to respond to and follow up on your enquiry, and we don't sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for marketing purposes.

Third-party services this site relies on

  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this site. No cookies are set for this.
  • Cloudflare — hosts this website and provides security/performance at the network level. No cookies are set for visitors by this.
  • Instagram and WhatsApp — links on this site take you to Instagram or WhatsApp's own apps or websites. We don't embed their tracking on this page; once you leave for their site or app, their own privacy policy applies.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information of yours we hold
  • Correct or update it
  • Delete it
  • Withdraw your consent to being contacted

To do any of this, email us at aetherinteriorsandfurnishings@gmail.com and we'll act on it promptly.

Grievance redressal

If you have a concern about how your data has been handled, contact us first at the email above. We'll do our best to resolve it directly and promptly.

Children's data

This website and its services are intended for adults arranging interior design or furniture work, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
