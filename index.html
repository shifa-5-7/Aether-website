About Us

Personal attention, custom craft, built around your budget.

Based out of Malad West, Aether Interiors & Furnishings is a specialized design studio handling everything from space planning to full site execution. We believe high-end interior design should be seamless, stress-free, and aligned with your personal financial comfort.

We don't do cookie-cutter templates. We work directly with homeowners across Mumbai to deliver completely tailored interiors. Whether you need a single room upgraded or a complete whole-house custom furniture setup — including wardrobes, modular kitchens, TV units, beds, and storage — everything is designed to your exact specifications, manufactured in our workshop, and personally supervised on-site.

  • 3D Design & Spatial PlanningScaled floor plans, interactive 3D site previews, and curated material boards.
  • Whole-House Customized FurnitureModular kitchens, walk-in wardrobes, custom beds, sofas, TV units & crockery cabinets built for your space.
  • Turnkey Site ExecutionFull on-site management including carpentry, electrical work, false ceilings, masonry & painting.
  • Furnishing & Final StylingHand-picked curtains, rugs, wallpaper, lighting fixtures & decorative accents.
